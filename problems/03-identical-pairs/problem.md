# 03 Pairs of identical integers (Pattern B)

A message of N positive integers is sent as pairs of identical integers. Print two numbers separated by a space: the number of pairs sent, and the number of integers left over.

Input: line 1 is N, line 2 is N integers.

Example
```
8
12 10 6 12 10 12 1 21
```
Output: 2 4  (12 12 and 10 10 are sent; 6 12 1 21 remain, which is 4)
